# Nhan's Vietnamese Specialty

•Base: 1 sheet of Vietnamese rice paper
• Binding/Sauce: 1 large egg (or 2–3 quail eggs)
• Aromatics & Toppings: 1–2 stalks green onions / spring onions (finely chopped), plus your choice of toppings (such as cooked minced pork, shredded ham, dried small shrimp, or cheese)
• Finishing Drizzle: Kewpie mayonnaise and Sriracha (or chili garlic sauce/sweet chili sauce)
